RanDoFit Support

RanDoFit gives you short workout prompts and optional reminders to move during your day.

Reminders are not appearing

  1. Open your device's Settings, find RanDoFit's notification settings, and check that notifications are allowed.
  2. Check whether Focus, Scheduled Summary or your lock-screen settings are silencing or delaying alerts.
  3. Open RanDoFit and schedule reminders again after checking your frequency and time window. Reminder schedules are finite and may need renewing.

Change or stop notifications

You can disable RanDoFit notifications in your device's Settings at any time. Available controls within the app depend on your installed version.

Choose a suitable workout

Choose the available difficulty and beginner settings that fit your needs. Each prompt shows its repetition count or duration. Skip or modify an exercise if it does not suit you.
