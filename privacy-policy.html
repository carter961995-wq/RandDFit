Privacy Policy

RanDoFit · Updated October 17, 2026

RanDoFit generates workout prompts and optional local reminders. The app does not require an account, and the developer does not collect personal information through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores preferences such as selected workout difficulties, beginner-friendly mode, reminder frequency, the reminder time window and onboarding status in its own local app storage. These preferences are used to provide the features you select.

Workout reminders

If you allow notifications, RanDoFit schedules workout reminders through Apple's notification system on your device. You can change or disable notification permission at any time in your device's Settings. Notifications may appear on your lock screen according to your device settings.

Collection, tracking and sharing

RanDoFit does not use a developer-operated account service, third-party advertising SDK or third-party analytics SDK. The developer does not sell your app data or use the app to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Apple services and this website

Apple processes App Store downloads, device backups and any diagnostics you choose to share under Apple's own policies and your device settings. This privacy page is hosted using GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy statement. This page contains no added advertising or analytics scripts.

Apple Privacy Policy · GitHub Privacy Statement

Your choices

You can adjust app preferences and notification settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data from that installation. Copies in a device backup may remain subject to your Apple backup settings.

If you contact the developer

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details or medical information.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated if the app's privacy practices change. Privacy questions can be sent to Logan Matthew Carter at carter961995@gmail.com.
