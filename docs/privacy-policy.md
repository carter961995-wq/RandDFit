# Privacy Policy

RandDFit does not sell your personal data.

## Data we collect

- **No account required**: RandDFit does not require sign-in.
- **On-device settings**: Your preferences (difficulty, reminder times) are stored locally on your device using iOS preferences storage.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, RandDFit schedules local notifications on your device. Notification content is generated on-device.
