## Description

Make room for a little movement with RanDoFit.

Get a short workout prompt when you want a fresh exercise idea. Choose your difficulty, use the beginner-friendly setting and see a clear repetition count or duration with an exercise illustration.

CHOOSE YOUR WORKOUT
Select Easy, Moderate or Hard exercises to match the challenge you want. Generate another prompt whenever you are ready for something different.

REMINDERS ON YOUR SCHEDULE
Set an optional daily reminder frequency and time window. Your device schedules the workout prompts locally, and you stay in control of notification permission. Reminder schedules are finite; reopen the app to renew them when needed.

SIMPLE BODYWEIGHT MOVEMENT
Explore short prompts built around exercises such as squats, lunges, planks and jumping jacks. Each workout states what to do and how many repetitions or how long to hold it.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
Your workout preferences stay in the app's local storage. Start with the settings that suit you and adjust them as you go.

Choose movements appropriate for you, and skip or modify exercises when needed.
